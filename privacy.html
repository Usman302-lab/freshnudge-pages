FreshNudge privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026. Applies to the FreshNudge Android app, version 1.0.
Who we are: the developer of FreshNudge ("we"). Contact: usmanarshadjutt302@gmail.com

The short version. FreshNudge helps you use food before it goes off. You create an account with your email address. Your food list is saved to your account so it is on every phone you sign in on. We show no ads, use no analytics or tracking, and do not sell or share your data. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, or by email.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, advertising ID or browsing activity, and the app contains no advertising or analytics code. The camera is used only while the scanner is open, only to read a barcode on your phone. No picture or video is taken, saved or sent.

Sharing a list with other people (households)

Everyone starts in a household of their own. If you invite someone, or join someone else's household with an invite code, everyone in that household can see and change its foods and see each other's nicknames. Nobody sees anyone's email address or password. Invite codes work once and expire after 7 days. Counters, reminder settings, your colour theme and your sign-in stay personal and are never shared.

When you leave or are removed, you start a new list of your own and the shared foods stay with the household. If the owner leaves or deletes their account, the longest-standing member becomes owner; if nobody is left, the household and its foods are deleted. Foods you added to a shared household stay there when you delete your account; they are no longer linked to you.

What stays only on your phone

  • Your sign-in (so you stay signed in), in the app's private storage.
  • A copy of your list, so the app works without a connection and opens instantly.
  • Your reminders. They are created on your phone from your list. No server sends them.

How we use your data

Only to run the app for you: sign you in, store and sync your list, keep the service secure, answer your support requests, and meet legal obligations. We do not use your data to profile you, to advertise, or to train any AI model.

Legal basis (EU and UK)

We handle your data to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract), for security and preventing abuse (our legitimate interests) and, for notifications, with your permission, which you can withdraw in your phone's settings at any time.

Who handles your data

  • Supabase (hosting, database and sign-in). Your account data is stored in the Frankfurt, Germany (eu-central-1) region. Supabase acts on our instructions as a data processor under its data processing addendum, which includes standard contractual clauses for transfers outside the EU and UK.
  • Google Play distributes the app. Google receives the data it needs to deliver the app to you, under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Open Food Facts (barcode lookups, optional). If you use Scan a barcode, your phone sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free, open food database, to find the product's name. Like any website, it can see your phone's IP address. We do not send your name, email address or account details, and your food list is not shared. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy applies to what it receives. If you never use the scanner, nothing is sent.
  • We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Where your data goes

Data is sent between your phone and our provider over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our provider may process data in countries outside your own, protected as described above.

Security

Each account can read and change only its own rows in our database. This is enforced by the database itself, not just by the app. The key built into the app cannot read other people's data, and our administrative key is never in the app. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use elsewhere.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete a food, its name is erased from our servers and only a small marker (an internal id and its dates) stays in your account, so your other phones learn about the deletion; that marker is removed when you delete your account. When you delete your account, your sign-in and all your foods and settings are deleted. Copies in our hosting provider's backups, if any, are removed on the provider's own schedule.

Your choices and rights

  • See and take your data: Settings, then Your data, then Save a backup creates a file of your list that you can keep.
  • Delete your foods: Settings, then Your data, then Delete all my data removes all foods, counts and settings from your account and every phone you use.
  • Delete your account: Settings, then Account, then Delete my account permanently deletes your account and everything in it. You can also ask us to do this without the app on our account deletion page or by emailing usmanarshadjutt302@gmail.com.
  • Your legal rights: depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your data, to object to how it is used, and to complain to your data protection authority. Email us and we will respond within the time the law requires.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, or set a reminder on a food, the app asks your phone for permission to show notifications. Reminder text can include food names; you can switch that off for the daily reminder in Settings. You can turn reminders off at any time. Reminders are made on your phone, not by our servers, and may arrive a few minutes after the time you chose.

Backup files you create

Save a backup makes a file and opens your phone's share sheet. Where it goes is your choice (for example Drive or email), and that service's own privacy policy applies. We do not receive the file. Deleting your account does not delete a file you saved elsewhere.

Phone permissions the app declares

Notifications (reminders), start-up completion (so reminders survive a restart), vibration (feedback when you tap), keeping the phone awake briefly (to schedule reminders), network access (to sign in, sync and look up barcodes) and the camera (only to scan barcodes, and only if you allow it; the app works fully without it). Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app.

Children

FreshNudge is not directed at children. You must be at least 13, or older where your country requires it, to create an account. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Features that are not live yet

We plan to add AI recipe suggestions and paid plans. None of these exist in this version, and none changes how your data is handled today. Before any of them launches, we will update this policy to say exactly what it involves (for example what is sent to create a recipe) and tell you in the app.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app. Questions or complaints: usmanarshadjutt302@gmail.com.
