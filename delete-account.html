Delete your FreshNudge account

Last updated: 5 November 2026

You can delete your FreshNudge account and everything in it. You do not need the app to ask.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open FreshNudge and tap Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: by email, without the app

  1. Send an email to usmanarshadjutt302@gmail.com with the subject Delete my FreshNudge account.
  2. Send it from the email address you signed up with. That is how we check the account is yours. If you cannot, tell us the address and we will reply with a way to confirm.
  3. We delete the account and reply to confirm, within 30 days and usually much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and your email address.
  • All your foods, places, reminders, settings and counts, on our servers and on every phone you use once it next connects.
  • Copies in our hosting provider's backups, if any, are removed on the provider's own schedule (see the privacy policy).

What is not deleted

  • If you shared a household list, foods you added to it stay with that household. They are no longer linked to you.
  • Backup files you saved yourself with Save a backup. They are on your own phone or in the service you chose, and we never received them.

Only want to remove your foods?

In the app, go to Settings, then Your data, then Delete all my data. This keeps your account but removes your foods, counts and settings.
